Foundations of Algorithms

Department of Computer Science and Engineering · IIT Kharagpur

Theme 01 · AI, Computing and Mathematics

Programme overview

A rigorous foundation in algorithm design and analysis, progressing from classical techniques to advanced topics. Covers exact, randomized, parameterized and approximation algorithms, with electives spanning algorithmic game theory, graph-theoretic methods and online decision-making.

Algorithms I and Algorithms II are core courses. The remaining credits are completed through electives selected from the list above.

Delivery and assessment

Delivery is blended, combining recorded lectures from IIT Kharagpur faculty with live interaction, on the ProofSlate managed platform. Assessment ordinarily combines continuous evaluation through weekly assignments and quizzes with an end-of-term evaluation, and is set and moderated by the Institute in every case.

One credit is equal to twelve contact hours. This programme carries 12 credits and 144 contact hours, and runs across one to three semesters or trimesters. Credits are aligned to NEP 2020, the National Credit Framework and the Academic Bank of Credits, so credits earned can be carried forward.

What qualifying learners earn

  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Micro-Specialisation Certificate upon successful completion
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Student Identity Card for the programme duration
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN email ID for the programme duration
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Alumni Status upon successful completion

All credentials are subject to IIT Kharagpur’s policies and conferred at the Institute’s sole discretion. They are benefits available to qualifying learners, never a guarantee.
