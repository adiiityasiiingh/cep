Admissions

How enrolment works, what is expected of a learner, and what the Institute confers on successful completion.

The route in

Five steps to enrolment

  1. Choose a Micro-Specialisation

    Browse the catalogue of 23 programmes across eight themes. Each entry records the academic unit, total credits, course count, contact hours, prerequisites and the full course stack.

  2. Check the prerequisites

    Prerequisites are set by the offering department and stated on each programme page — from none at all to specific subject knowledge.

  3. Register interest

    Submit an enquiry through the form on this page, or through your institution if it is an OCN partner college. Learners at partner institutions are onboarded within their existing programme of study.

  4. Confirm enrolment

    On confirmation of the offering, learners are enrolled on the ProofSlate managed platform and issued an IIT Kharagpur OCN email ID and Student Identity Card for the programme duration.

  5. Study, be assessed, be certified

    Complete the course stack across one to three terms under continuous and end-of-term evaluation set and moderated by the Institute, and earn the OCN Micro-Specialisation Certificate and Alumni Status.

Who the Network is for

Students at partner colleges, working professionals, faculty of science, engineering and technical colleges, and R&D practitioners seeking credit-bearing specialisation from IIT Kharagpur.

Credit portability

Aligned to NEP 2020, the National Credit Framework and the Academic Bank of Credits. Credits earned can be carried forward.

Before you apply

Programme names, credits, course stacks, faculty allocation, semester scheduling and availability are indicative and subject to academic finalisation by the respective departments. Confirmed offerings are notified through IIT Kharagpur’s official channels.

Questions

Frequently asked

Eligibility is set per programme by the offering department. Most programmes state their prerequisites explicitly — some require none at all, while others assume first-year foundational mathematics, a programming language, or subject knowledge such as genetics or digital communication. The prerequisite for each programme is recorded on its page in the catalogue.

Programmes are aligned to NEP 2020, the National Credit Framework and the Academic Bank of Credits, so credits earned can be carried forward. Recognition against a learner’s own degree remains a matter for their parent institution.

Assessment ordinarily combines continuous evaluation through weekly assignments and quizzes with an end-of-term evaluation. Assessments are set and moderated by IIT Kharagpur in every case. Several programmes additionally carry a capstone or laboratory component.

Delivery is blended: recorded lectures from IIT Kharagpur faculty combined with live interaction, on the ProofSlate managed platform. Laboratory and capstone components are scheduled by the offering department and any campus requirement is notified with the programme.

Qualifying learners earn an IIT Kharagpur OCN Micro-Specialisation Certificate on successful completion, together with an OCN Student Identity Card and an IIT Kharagpur OCN email ID for the programme duration, and OCN Alumni Status on successful completion. All credentials are subject to IIT Kharagpur’s policies and conferred at the Institute’s sole discretion. They are benefits available to qualifying learners, never a guarantee.

Programmes run across one to three semesters or trimesters. Total credits are typically twelve or more, with one credit equal to twelve contact hours — so a twelve-credit programme carries 144 contact hours.

Fees are set per programme and notified with the confirmed offering through IIT Kharagpur’s official channels. Learners enrolling through a partner institution should consult that institution, as programmes may be delivered within an existing programme of study.

IIT Kharagpur OCN Outreach Course Network

The Platinum Jubilee Outreach Course Network is IIT Kharagpur’s Board-approved initiative for extending credit-bearing academic programmes beyond its campus, governed through the Office of the Dean (Outreach).
