Credit-bearing Micro-Specialisations from IIT Kharagpur

The Outreach Course Network extends the Institute’s academic programmes beyond its campus. Every Micro-Specialisation is a structured stack of courses authored by an IIT Kharagpur department or school, delivered by its faculty, and assessed and certified under the Institute’s authority.

How to read the catalogue

A common construct across every programme

Credits are typically twelve or more, with one credit equal to twelve contact hours. Programmes run across one to three semesters or trimesters, and credits earned can be carried forward.

Authored by the Institute

Each stack is authored by an IIT Kharagpur department, school or centre of excellence, and taught by its own faculty.

One to three terms

Programmes are scheduled across one to three semesters or trimesters, designed to sit alongside an existing degree or a working week.

Credits that travel

Aligned to NEP 2020, the National Credit Framework and the Academic Bank of Credits, so credits earned can be carried forward.

Institute-moderated assessment

Continuous evaluation through weekly assignments and quizzes with an end-of-term evaluation, set and moderated by the Institute in every case.

Delivery and governance

Three layers, clearly separated

The Network is governed through the Office of the Dean (Outreach). Academic authority never leaves the Institute.

Layer 01

Academic authority

IIT Kharagpur designs the courses, delivers faculty, sets and moderates assessments, and certifies learners. All intellectual property in the programme content, curriculum and teaching materials vests in the Institute.

Layer 02

Orchestration

ProofSlate, the designated orchestration and governance partner, operates the platform, learner operations, settlement and governance — the entire non-academic layer.

Layer 03

Delivery partners

Institutions and platforms market the programmes and mobilise learners within the OCN framework, under structured, credit-compatible partnerships.
