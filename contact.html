Getting to Kharagpur

The Institute’s campus lies at Kharagpur in West Bengal, on the South Eastern Railway, roughly 120 kilometres west of Kolkata. Kharagpur Junction is the nearest railway station and Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport, Kolkata, the nearest airport.
