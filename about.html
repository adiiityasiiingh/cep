About the Outreach Course Network

IIT Kharagpur’s Board-approved initiative for extending credit-bearing academic programmes beyond its campus, governed through the Office of the Dean (Outreach).

The initiative

Outreach as a strategic instrument

The Platinum Jubilee Outreach Course Network (OCN) is IIT Kharagpur’s Board-approved initiative for extending credit-bearing academic programmes beyond its campus. It marks the Institute’s strategic shift to democratise premier technical education — outreach treated not as an adjunct to the academic mission but as an instrument of it.

The Network establishes structural partnerships directly with regional colleges. Partner colleges receive structured learning frameworks under direct IIT Kharagpur faculty mentorship, academic supervision and verified credit systems, with the Network developing structured, credit-compatible partnerships with 200 or more colleges.

What a Micro-Specialisation is

A Micro-Specialisation is a structured stack of courses authored by an IIT Kharagpur department or school, delivered by its faculty, and assessed and certified under the Institute’s authority. Every programme in the catalogue follows the same construct.

  • Credits. Typically twelve or more, with one credit equal to twelve contact hours.
  • Duration. One to three semesters or trimesters.
  • Portability. Aligned to NEP 2020, the National Credit Framework and the Academic Bank of Credits, so credits earned can be carried forward.
  • Delivery. Blended — recorded lectures from IIT Kharagpur faculty combined with live interaction, on the ProofSlate managed platform.
  • Assessment. Ordinarily continuous evaluation through weekly assignments and quizzes with an end-of-term evaluation, set and moderated by the Institute in every case.
  • Applied work. Several programmes carry a capstone or laboratory component.

Each catalogue entry records the academic unit, total credits, course count, contact hours, prerequisites and the full course stack.

Governance

The Network is governed through the Office of the Dean (Outreach). Academic authority, curriculum, evaluation and certification rest entirely with IIT Kharagpur, and all intellectual property in the programme content, curriculum and teaching materials vests in the Institute.

The Institute

Established in 1951 as the first of the Indian Institutes of Technology, IIT Kharagpur is an Institute of National Importance. Its Platinum Jubilee year marks seventy-five years of teaching and research, and the Network carries that academic authority outward — from the departments of Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics and Mechanical Engineering to the G. S. Sanyal School of Telecommunications, the Cryogenic Engineering Centre, the School of Energy Science and Engineering and the Rajendra Mishra School of Engineering Entrepreneurship.
