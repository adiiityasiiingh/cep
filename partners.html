Partners

The Network establishes structural partnerships directly with regional colleges, under direct IIT Kharagpur faculty mentorship, academic supervision and verified credit systems.

How the Network is organised

Three layers, clearly separated

Academic authority never leaves the Institute. Everything else is organised around it.

Layer 01

Academic authority

IIT Kharagpur designs the courses, delivers faculty, sets and moderates assessments, and certifies learners. All intellectual property in the programme content, curriculum and teaching materials vests in the Institute.

Governed through the Office of the Dean (Outreach).

Layer 02

Orchestration

ProofSlate is the designated orchestration and governance partner for the OCN, under formal agreements with IIT Kharagpur. It operates the non-academic layer end to end: partner onboarding, compliance, settlement, credentialing workflows, digital infrastructure and brand governance.

Curriculum, delivery, evaluation and certification rest entirely with IIT Kharagpur.

Layer 03

Delivery partners

Institutions and platforms market the programmes and mobilise learners within the OCN framework. Partner colleges receive structured learning frameworks under direct IIT Kharagpur faculty mentorship, academic supervision and verified credit systems.

The Network is developing credit-compatible partnerships with 200+ colleges.

Official Orchestration Partner ProofSlate Excellence. Networked.
For institutions

Becoming a partner college

Partnership places IIT Kharagpur’s credit-bearing Micro-Specialisations inside your existing programme of study.

  1. Expression of interest

    Your institution writes to the Office of the Dean (Outreach) setting out its programmes, learner cohort and the themes of interest.

  2. Academic and institutional review

    The Institute reviews academic fit, cohort readiness and the credit-compatibility of the proposed placement within your curriculum.

  3. Onboarding and agreement

    Partner onboarding, compliance and the partnership instrument are administered through the orchestration layer. Commercial terms and partnership instruments are subject to mutual agreement and requisite approvals.

  4. Cohort delivery

    Learners are enrolled on the managed platform, taught by IIT Kharagpur faculty through blended delivery, and assessed under Institute moderation.

What a partner college receives

  • Structured learning frameworks authored by IIT Kharagpur academic units
  • Direct IIT Kharagpur faculty mentorship and academic supervision
  • Verified credit systems aligned to NEP 2020, NCrF and the ABC
  • Credit-bearing Micro-Specialisations placed within the existing curriculum
  • Institute-set and Institute-moderated assessment

What learners at a partner college receive

  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Micro-Specialisation Certificate on successful completion
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Student Identity Card for the programme duration
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN email ID for the programme duration
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Alumni Status on successful completion

Subject to IIT Kharagpur’s policies and conferred at the Institute’s sole discretion. Benefits available to qualifying learners, never a guarantee.

Brand and licence

Use of the Institute’s marks

The IIT Kharagpur name, logo and marks are used solely within the scope of OCN programmes, under licence and subject to the Institute’s brand governance. Their use does not imply endorsement of any partner organisation’s own programmes or offerings.

All IIT Kharagpur OCN credentials are subject to the Institute’s policies and conferred at its sole discretion.
