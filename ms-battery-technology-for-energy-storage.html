Battery Technology for Energy Storage

School of Energy Science and Engineering · IIT Kharagpur

Theme 03 · Energy, Sustainability and Climate

Programme overview

A broad overview of energy storage technologies, comparing batteries, fuel cells and alternative systems. Covers the fundamentals of battery operation, key components and performance metrics, followed by rechargeable battery behaviour, modelling and simulation.

Course stack — Battery Technology for Energy Storage
CourseL-T-PCredits
Battery Storage and Fuel Cells 3-1-0 4
Rechargeable Battery Performance Modeling 3-0-0 3
Capstone Project 0-0-6 6

Delivery and assessment

Delivery is blended, combining recorded lectures from IIT Kharagpur faculty with live interaction, on the ProofSlate managed platform. Assessment ordinarily combines continuous evaluation through weekly assignments and quizzes with an end-of-term evaluation, and is set and moderated by the Institute in every case.

One credit is equal to twelve contact hours. This programme carries 13 credits and 156 contact hours, and runs across one to three semesters or trimesters. Credits are aligned to NEP 2020, the National Credit Framework and the Academic Bank of Credits, so credits earned can be carried forward.

What qualifying learners earn

  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Micro-Specialisation Certificate upon successful completion
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Student Identity Card for the programme duration
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN email ID for the programme duration
  • IIT Kharagpur OCN Alumni Status upon successful completion

All credentials are subject to IIT Kharagpur’s policies and conferred at the Institute’s sole discretion. They are benefits available to qualifying learners, never a guarantee.
